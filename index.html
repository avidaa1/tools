Stylized rendering of the Old Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) positional numeral system: a unit wedge repeated up to 9 times, a ten-corner (Winkelhaken) repeated up to 5 times, per digit position. Educational rendering, not a full logographic cuneiform converter.

Household Chemical Safety

A fixed reference over 8 common household products - the same "never mix" warnings printed on product labels and published by poison-control services. This is a closed lookup, not a general chemical combination tool: pick two products, get the documented hazard if one exists, or the general safety rule if it doesn't. Nothing about ignition, fuels, or combustion is in scope here.

General rule regardless of the pair: never combine cleaning products, use one at a time, ventilate, and keep original labels visible. US Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.

SCIENCE BENCH

Standard chemistry and biology curriculum tools - atomic bonding, cell structure, protein chains, and DNA base-pairing. All computed from real reference data (Pauling electronegativity, standard amino acid codes, Watson-Crick base pairing), not decorative.

Atom Bonding

Bond type is determined from the real electronegativity difference between the two elements (Pauling scale): over 1.7 is ionic, under that is covalent. Ionic formulas are balanced from real valence electron counts. Covalent formulas are shown only for a curated set of well-known compounds - unlisted pairs show the general sharing tendency without guessing a formula.

Cell Builder

Toggle animal or plant, then add organelles one at a time. Plant-only and animal-only organelles are filtered automatically - a plant cell view will not offer a centriole, an animal view will not offer a chloroplast.

Protein / Peptide Bond Builder

Enter a sequence of standard single-letter amino acid codes. Each bond between amino acids is a real peptide bond, formed by dehydration synthesis (releasing one water molecule per bond).

DNA Base-Pairing Builder

Enter a sequence of A, T, C, G. The complementary strand is generated from real Watson-Crick base-pairing rules (A pairs with T, G pairs with C), plus GC content and a short-sequence melting-temperature estimate (Wallace rule, valid under 14 bases).
